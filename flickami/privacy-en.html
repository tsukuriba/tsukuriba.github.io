Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Flickami does not collect personal data and contains no ads or analytics. The app connects to the internet only when you send something with “Send feedback” (see below).

Data we collect

None, except what you choose to send with “Send feedback”. The app does not include:

  • Connections to external servers, other than when you send something with “Send feedback”
  • Ads or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Analytics or measurement tools (including Firebase Analytics)
  • Account registration or sign-in
  • Access to location, contacts or the microphone
  • Automatic crash reports sent off the device

Camera and Photos

  • Camera: used only when you scan a friend's team QR code with “Team QR”. The image is read on your device and is neither saved nor sent anywhere. You can play without the camera (you can also paste the code instead).
  • Photos: used only when you choose “Save image” under “Share as image”, to save a picture of your team to Photos. The app never reads your photos.

Your device asks for permission before either is used. Declining does not affect the rest of the game.

Send feedback

Only when you send it from Settings → “Send feedback”, the app sends the following, all shown on screen first, to the maker's (Tsukuriba's) Google Form. Nothing is sent if you don't send it.

  • The note you write (optional)
  • App version, device model and OS version, and language setting
  • Where you were (such as the stage), your progress, team and settings
  • Whether the app ended unexpectedly last time
  • The play log: the last 200 lines the app has on hand, up to 20,000 characters (game events such as stage, shot angle and damage, plus readings of how the game is running, such as frame rate and processing time). One first line with the app version, device, language and place is added

How it is sent differs slightly by device.

  • Android: tapping “Send” makes the app send it straight to the form. The message is kept to about 42 KB, so a long log may lose its oldest lines (the first line is kept).
  • iPhone and iPad: tapping “Open the form to send” opens a pre-filled feedback form in Safari or your browser. It reaches the maker only when you tap “Submit” in the browser. (Opening the form delivers the address containing the pre-filled text to Google.) A note or log too long to fit in the address is copied to your device's clipboard so you can paste it in; whether to paste it is up to you.

No name, email address, account, contact details, device identifier or location is sent. What you send cannot be used to identify you. It is used only to fix bugs and improve the game, and is never shared with third parties. Because it is a Google Form, it passes through and is stored on Google's servers (Google Privacy Policy).

What is stored on your device

Your play data (progress, allies, points and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere, except what you send with “Send feedback”. Deleting the app also deletes this data.

Children

There is no age restriction. Because no personal data is collected, no personal information is gathered even when children under 13 play without parental consent (“Send feedback” never sends a name or contact details either).

Sharing with third parties

We collect no personal data. What you send with “Send feedback” is never shared with third parties.

Music

The background music comes from OpenTracks (formerly DOVA-SYNDROME, https://opentracks.com/) and is used under that site's license. The music is built into the app and cannot be extracted as audio files.
