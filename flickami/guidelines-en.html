Fan Content Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

We welcome wikis, guides, videos, streams and fan art that use Flickami's screens, art and numbers. Most uses need no permission and no message. This page sets out the rules so you can create with confidence.

The short version

Go ahead (no permission, no message needed)

  • Screenshots, gameplay videos, Let's Plays and live streams
  • Ad revenue from videos and streams (through the monetization tools of YouTube, Twitch, Niconico and similar services)
  • Tips, memberships, sponsorships, affiliate income and paid articles (as long as you aren't selling the game's material itself)
  • Wikis, guides and roundups, including sites with ads. Names, numbers and description text of allies, abilities, specials and stages
  • Copying tables from the game data tables
  • Using the logo, icon, key art and screenshots from the press kit in articles, videos and introductions (crops and resizing are fine)
  • Fan art, comics, stories, videos and other fan works that you do not charge for
  • Small-scale doujinshi and merchandise (handmade or small runs, at fan events, personal shops or BOOTH)

Please ask first

  • Apps, games and other derivative works that use the game's material
  • A company or organization using the game's material to promote its own products or services (news and review coverage is free, as on the press kit page); organizing tournaments or events
  • Fan works funded through crowdfunding
  • Using the name “Flickami” in a channel name, account name, domain name or app name

Please don't

  • Extract files (art, sound, data) from the app to use or share them
  • Share, sell or upload the music or sound effects on their own as audio
  • Register videos that contain the game's music with Content ID or similar rights-claim systems
  • Pass yourself off as official (claiming to be official, or confusing people with the logo)
  • Publish unreleased content found by analyzing the app
  • Break the law or others' rights, hurt people, or promote cheating or modding
  • Mass-produce fan works for sale, sell them through large marketplaces or print-on-demand shops, or use them in NFTs or cryptocurrency
  • Use the game's art, sound, text or data to train or fine-tune generative AI

1 Screens, videos and streams

Screenshots, gameplay videos, Let's Plays, live streams and short videos need no permission and no message. It doesn't matter where you post them.

You may earn ad revenue on videos and streams through the monetization tools that YouTube, Twitch, Niconico and similar services provide.

Besides ad revenue, you may earn tips (Super Chat and the like), channel memberships, sponsorships, affiliate income and paid articles (such as on note) without asking. You may not sell Flickami's art, sound or data tables themselves, or put them behind a paywall (the content must be your own commentary, play or guide).

2 Wikis, guides and roundups

  • You may post names, numbers and description text for allies, abilities, specials, vows and stages as they are. You may also copy the game data tables.
  • Sites with ads or affiliate links are fine.
  • Please credit the source (a request; no need to tell us). For example:
    Source: Flickami official data tables (https://tsukuriba.github.io/flickami/data/), version 1.0.5
  • The data changes with each version. Saying which version it is from keeps readers from getting confused.
  • A name like “Flickami Guide Wiki” that clearly says it is unofficial is fine. Please don't use names or domains that look like the official site.

3 Art, logo and materials

  • The logo, icon, key art and screenshots in the press kit are free to use in articles, videos and introductions. Crops and resizing are fine.
  • Please don't redraw the logo to look official, or alter its colors or letters to pass it off as a different logo.
  • Screenshots you take of the game and gameplay videos need no permission and no message.
  • Please don't use art, sound or data files taken from inside the app, and don't analyze or modify the app to extract them.

4 Music and sound effects

Flickami's music and sound effects include material that creators have licensed to us. The music comes from OpenTracks (formerly DOVA-SYNDROME), as noted under “Music” in the Privacy Policy. That is why the sound has these rules:

  • Gameplay videos, Let's Plays and streams that include the game's sound are fine.
  • You may not share, sell or upload the audio on its own, including videos where the sound is the main content (“study music”, “soundtrack” and the like).
  • Please don't register videos that contain the game's music with Content ID or similar rights-claim systems. The source of the music forbids such registration. Tsukuriba will not register Flickami videos in these systems either.
  • If a copyright claim is placed on your video, please contact us.

5 Fan art and fan works

Fan art, comics, stories, videos, cosplay and similar works that you don't charge for need no permission and no message. We ask that you:

  • Make clear that it is a fan work and not official. For example:
    An unofficial Flickami fan work. Flickami is a work by Tsukuriba.
  • Don't use the game's art or sound files as they are inside your work (screenshots are fine).
  • Don't make things that badly damage the feel of the game, hurt people, or are excessively sexual or violent.

For works you sell (doujinshi, merchandise, art books and so on): handmade or small-run items sold at fan events, through personal online shops or on sites like BOOTH need no message. Mass production, sale through large marketplaces or large print-on-demand shops, and use in NFTs or cryptocurrency are not allowed.

Please ask first about apps, games and other derivative works that fans make. The game's art and sound can't be used in them.

We may feature wonderful works on our official accounts. If you would rather we didn't, please tell us.

6 Avoiding the look of an official work

  • Please don't claim to be official, or make your channel or account look like an official contact.
  • You may use the name “Flickami” in titles and descriptions of introductions, guides and Let's Plays. Please ask first before using it in a channel name, account name, domain name or app name, because people may mistake it for official.
  • Adding the unofficial notice from the example above to your work or video description helps avoid confusion.

7 Generative AI

Please don't use Flickami's art, sound, text or data to train or fine-tune generative AI (including additional training and LoRA). Fan art and videos made with AI may be published as long as you say they were made with AI.

8 Unreleased information

Please don't publish content found by analyzing the app's files that has not been released yet (allies, stages or features that may be added later). It is often different from what ships, and it takes away the fun for players.

9 Other requests

  • Follow the law and respect others' rights. Please don't defame people or groups or expose personal information.
  • Please don't promote cheating, modding or unfair ways of getting things.
  • Nothing here limits uses the law allows, such as quotation under copyright law.

10 Takedowns, changes and liability

  • If a use doesn't follow these rules, or we judge it damages the game's name, we may ask you to take it down or fix it. We would contact you first, and fixing it is usually enough.
  • Not asking about something does not mean we approve of it.
  • When we change these rules we will update this page and its “Last updated” date.
  • Tsukuriba is not responsible for trouble or damage arising from fan works or other uses.
