Mix and match abilities. Flick to fight.

Pull a medal back with your finger and let go. It banks off the walls and crashes into the enemy. That's the whole gesture — your angle, your pull and where you stop decide the next move.

Features

270+ allies, abilities to combine

Every ally has its own ability, and you can equip abilities from allies of the same element to stack several on one character. Five elements, a special move for every ally, and many SSR allies awaken into their true forms.

Call your shot

"Finish it this shot." "Bank off three walls." Declare it before you fire and land it for a big payoff. Land all four kinds and three allies fuse into a single medal.

Bind yourself, grow stronger

"No special moves" for 1.9× attack. "Can't shoot while above half HP" for 2.4×. Break a vow and that battle's bonus is gone.

Stack abilities, take out three in one shot

260+ abilities to equip and combine. Pair an ally whose shots split off copies with one that bursts defeated foes in four directions, and one shot can take out three.

55 stages, 165 quests

Sixteen kinds of board tricks and five ailments reshape every stage. Clear quests to recruit new allies, then climb the Endless Tower.

Share teams by QR

Show your team to friends as a QR code or an image — or tap Auto to build one for the next stage's enemies.
