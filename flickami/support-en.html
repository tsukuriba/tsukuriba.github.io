FAQ

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
The game has no built-in transfer feature (no accounts or transfer codes). Your progress is stored on your device, and deleting the app also deletes it.

Does it cost anything?
It's a one-time purchase. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does it ask for the camera?
Only to scan a friend's team QR code. You can play without allowing it.
