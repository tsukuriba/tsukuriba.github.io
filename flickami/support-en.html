FAQ

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
The game has no built-in transfer feature (no accounts or transfer codes). Your progress is stored on your device, and deleting the app also deletes it.

Does it cost anything?
It's a one-time purchase. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

Why does it ask for the camera?
Only to scan a friend's team QR code. You can play without allowing it.

Where can I read how to play?
Open Settings and tap "Help (how to play)" to read the battle and menu tip cards at any time.

How do I switch the language?
At the top of Settings, under Language, choose Auto, 日本語 or English.

There's no sound
Check the BGM and sound effect volumes in Settings, and your device's volume and silent mode.

Can I get a refund?
Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later. About 1 GB of free storage is needed. An Android version is in preparation.
