When you can't win

I keep losing a stage

Try these four, in order.

  1. Drills: In Team, pick an ally and open Train. Drills raise ATK and HP with pt. Points tend to pile up, and putting them into drills is the quickest way to get stronger. Lv goes up as allies fight in battles.
  2. Hit weaknesses: Fire beats Leaf beats Water beats Fire; Light and Dark beat each other. A weakness hit deals ×1.5 damage, and a "!" shows when you land one.
  3. Rethink your team: With only one element, some foes have no weakness you can hit. With only low-HP allies, a boss attack can take out two at once.
  4. Use Auto: Auto in Team builds a team for the next stage's foes. You can go back to your team from before Auto. Stage cards have Auto too.

When you lose, one line tells you why and what to try next. Tap "Get stronger" to go straight to Team.

It says I'm short of the recommended Lv

Replay earlier stages to raise Lv, or make up the gap with drills. The recommended Lv is a guide — you can sometimes win a little below it.

Where can I read how to play again?

Open Settings and tap "Help (how to play)" to read the battle and menu tip cards at any time. Hold a highlighted word to see what it means.

pt and Pull

What is pt for?

Two things: Pull and drills. You earn pt in battles. Even when you lose, you keep 30% of the pt from that battle. pt is an in-game currency and can't be bought with money (there are no in-app purchases).

  • Pull: 300 pt for one, 2,700 pt for ten. Your first 10 pulls cost no pt.
  • Drills: raise ATK and HP for that ally only. Bulk Training drills several allies at once.

What are the Pull rates?

SSR is 8.9% (the real rate, including the 15-pull guarantee). After 15 pulls with no SSR, the next one is an SSR. Tap "Drop rates →" on the Pull screen to check at any time.

In 1.0.7 the screen shows 9.3%. That number was miscounted — the real rate is 8.9%, and 1.0.8 fixes the text.

Sending feedback

How do I send feedback or a bug report?

In the game, open Settings and tap "Send feedback" to send a short note and your play log without your name. You can also open it from the Menu during a battle. What is sent is described in the Privacy Policy.

You can also email us at tsukuriba.support+flickami@gmail.com. It helps if you include your device model, your iOS version, and what happened.

Devices and progress

Is there an Android version?

It's in preparation. For now you can play on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Can I play on a Mac?

Yes, on a Mac with Apple silicon, from the Mac App Store. Progress is saved per device and isn't shared between your iPhone and your Mac.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

The game has no built-in transfer feature (no accounts or transfer codes). Your progress is stored on your device, and deleting the app also deletes it.
